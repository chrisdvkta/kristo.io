One of the best action thrillers I've watched so far.

Tony Montana started out as a hungry Cuban willing to do absolutely anything it took to get to the top.

Tony started out as an illegal immigrant in Miami who was willing to do anything to get a green card and freely roam around the States.

He did a few favors for Frank Lopez, which ultimately got him and his brother Manny green cards and brought him closer to Frank's business.

Tony wanted everything Frank had, and he ultimately got it. He had a real hunger, unlike Frank, who had money but had no balls and let others do the dirty work for him.

The real turning point of the movie came when Tony finally got everything he wanted: a big house, a shit ton of fuck-you money, the girl he wanted, an expanded distribution network, and connections.

However, the hunger that the illegal Cuban had was gone. The cocaine and hedonism got to him. He couldn't keep his ego in check.

"Are you the same person in praise and insult?" Tony failed to live up to it.

His ego only got bigger. He credited himself, and only himself, for the Montana empire, completely disregarding his day ones like Manny and making them feel smaller.

In a way, he was turning into the very thing he disliked about Frank: a fucking loser. (It's the nu-core.)

His complete disregard for the chain of command ultimately cost him his operations, the only thing he loved unconditionally; his sister; and ultimately, his life.

The movie, for me, was perfectly paced. Nothing was rushed, and the movie ended exactly how it should have. There was no other ending for Tony.

8/10

On a completely unrelated note: nu-core may be one of my best discoveries in recent times. I've always liked drums.
